Streaming the golden age of early cinema: 1900s–1910s

Fantasmagorie (1908)

The world's first fully animated film by Émile Cohl.

Released in 1908, Fantasmagorie is widely considered by film historians to be the world's first fully animated film.

"A Trip to the Moon" (1902)

Georges Méliès' landmark sci-fi masterpiece.

Released in 1902, A Trip to the Moon is widely regarded as the earliest science fiction film.

"The Great Train Robbery" (1903)

Edwin S. Porter's pioneering Western action film.

Directed by Edwin S. Porter in 1903, this landmark silent film established the narrative blueprint for the American Western genre.

"The Perils of Pauline" - Episode 1 (1914)

The classic cliffhanger serial starring Pearl White.

Released in 1914, this legendary action serial cemented the enduring cultural trope of the endangered heroine.

"Regeneration" (1915)

Raoul Walsh's gritty landmark gangster drama.

Directed by Raoul Walsh in 1915, Regeneration is considered one of the earliest feature-length gangster films.

"Intolerance" (1916)

D.W. Griffith's monumental historical epic.

Released in 1916, Intolerance remains a marvel of silent cinema scale.

"The Immigrant" (1917)

Charlie Chaplin's classic comedy short.

Created in 1917, The Immigrant blends physical comedy with social commentary.

"The Portage Trail" (c. 1915)

A silent melodrama featuring early 20th-century Akron, Ohio.

This silent film tells a romance story against the backdrop of Akron, Ohio.
